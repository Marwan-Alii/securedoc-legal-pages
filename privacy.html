Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy describes how SecureDoc is intended to handle information when people use the SecureDoc document distribution application.

Important: SecureDoc is under development. The practices described here must be checked against the deployed application's actual behavior before the service is opened to users.

1. Information that may be processed

Depending on the features used, SecureDoc may process account and profile information, document metadata, access permissions, activity records, and documents uploaded by users. The specific information collected depends on the deployed version and its configuration.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to provide document distribution, manage access permissions, maintain service functionality, troubleshoot errors, and protect the application against misuse.

3. Document storage and security

SecureDoc is being designed with document protection and controlled access in mind. Its deployed security depends on the implementation and configuration of the application and its service providers. Users should not upload confidential or sensitive documents until the service's security and storage practices have been verified.

4. Third-party services

The deployed service may use third-party infrastructure providers for hosting, database storage, or document storage. Those providers may process information as necessary to deliver their services and under their applicable terms and privacy policies.

5. Sharing and disclosure

SecureDoc does not intend to sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers or disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users. Actual practices must be verified against the deployed application.

6. Data retention and deletion

Information may be retained while needed to operate the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce applicable policies. Retention and deletion procedures depend on the deployed implementation.

7. Children's privacy

SecureDoc is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws. Contact the service operator if you have a concern about information associated with a child.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as SecureDoc develops. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised update date when needed.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the support contact provided on the SecureDoc application's official website.
